FAQ

Common questions.

Do I need my own domain?

No. If you don't have a domain yet, we can set one up for you as part of your website. If you already own a domain, we're happy to connect it to your new site.

Can I switch from renting to owning?

Yes. Many customers start with a website rental and later upgrade to full ownership once they're ready. We'll walk you through the transition and what's included.

How long does setup take?

Most websites are designed, customized, and ready to launch within a short turnaround after we receive your business details and any photos or branding you'd like to include.

Will my website work on phones?

Yes. Every website we build is fully responsive and designed to look and work great on phones, tablets, and desktops.

Can I request updates?

Yes. Rental customers can request updates to their content, and custom-owned websites can be updated through an optional maintenance plan.

Do you provide hosting?

Yes, hosting is included with every website rental. Hosting for owned websites can be included or handled separately depending on your preference.

Can you redesign my existing website?

Yes. We can rebuild an existing website with a fresh, modern design while keeping the information customers rely on.
